01Thesisgoal & context

Sound from a listed app must not stay muted while the person is on the space. A mute that outlives its record is never released today, because the plugin only unmutes what its record names. A muted stream inside the distraction slice while the hold is off is far more likely an orphan than a choice.

Two additions to the mute module. Every mute, unmute, dropped record, and failed pactl call writes one log line. When the hold ends, and once at listener start while the hold is off, the release pass also unmutes any muted stream whose process is inside the slice, record or no record. Streams outside the slice keep the record-only rule.

03Edge cases & constraints4 items
accepted trade-off
A mute the person set on a distraction stream while on the space is released at the next hold end.
membership
The slice check reuses the cgroup test the plugin already applies when it decides what to mute.
exit
A listener that stops while the hold is on still releases on exit; the sweep covers the case where that did not happen.
log volume
One line per action; a rescan that changes nothing writes nothing.
04Boundaries
this spec is
  • A slice-wide sweep at release time
  • A log line per mute action
  • Tests for both
this spec is not
  • A change to what gets muted, when the hold applies, or the identity check
  • Adoption of orphaned mutes outside the slice
05Decision context
D1Sweep the slice at release time.
Fixes the orphan without weakening the promise that the plugin never touches a mute outside its own boundary; the slice is that boundary.
Rejected: claiming every pre-muted slice stream into the record at start (same effect, more state); logging alone (diagnosable but still stuck).
