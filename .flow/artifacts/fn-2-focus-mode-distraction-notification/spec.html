01Thesisscope: business

While focus mode is on, distraction-space apps send no banner and no sound. Other apps keep notifying. Focus off lifts this mute, then one grouped per-app count may play one sound.

Mechanism is a plugin Quickshell service bound to the live shell.serviceFor("omarchy.notifications") plus the sole session stream watcher owned by listen. Whole-desktop toggleDnd (Super+Ctrl+,) stays the user mute and is out of spec for R2.

04Architecturescope: technical

Live Quickshell service

Lazily binds shell.serviceFor("omarchy.notifications"). An invisible Instantiator captures immutable keys, then Qt.callLater relocates, revalidates, and removes only that exact row/ref after queuing its popup-file delete.

One acknowledged watcher

The session-long listen process solely owns pactl subscribe. Toggles write a monotonic generation and wait for matching armed/disarmed status; they never spawn a fallback watcher.

Canonical identity + count

notification-members.json drives QML and Python. Count helpers share a flock, fsync, and atomic rename; lift waits for disarmed-and-drained before one notice and successful clear.

05Edgesapply / identity / compose

Chromium identity

Banners accept only an anchored leading body origin and exact shipped host. Sounds require a host/app-id in bounded, PID-guarded process ancestry. Generic browser identity never matches.

Apply fail

Tell the user. Roll back this spec's mutation. Focus still turns on.

Boundary races

Deferred suppression rechecks row identity and live ref. Drain-before-read plus the shared count lock makes a boundary ping counted or visible, never cleared unseen.

06Boundariesscope: business

Is

  • Mute banners and sounds from shipped workspace apps while focus is on
  • Keep notifications from every other app
  • Restore, then one grouped per-app count

Is not

  • Network destination blocking (sibling fn-1)
  • Whole-desktop toggleDnd, mako, or SwayNC
  • History screen, allow-list, urgent bypass, all-Chrome mute
07Decisionsplan
D1 · daemon

Omarchy 4 exposes its live NotificationServer service in the same QML engine.

Chosen: bind manifest id omarchy.notifications, require its exact-row APIs, queue deletion behind persistence, then remove and dismiss only the captured row/ref.

Rejected: dismiss(summary), whole-desktop DND, a second NotificationServer, mako, and SwayNC.

D2 · membership

"i don't want to get a notification from apps in distraction space"

Chosen: one canonical notification-members.json, parity-tested with named window rules. Native identities use app/icon and Pulse keys; PWA banners use an anchored leading body origin, while sounds use process ancestry.

Rejected: generic browser app/icon/desktop-entry, all-Chromium mute, and runtime Super+Alt+D strays.

D3 · catch-up

Grouped per-app count after focus off. May play one sound.

Chosen: increment only after exact suppression is queued; serialize helpers; share a count-file flock; disarm and drain before locked read/notice/clear.

Rejected: replay each banner. Rejected: mid-focus history (declined extra UI).

D4 · watcher ownership

New streams must be caught without duplicate owners or detached fallbacks.

Chosen: listen alone owns the long-lived pactl worker; focus toggles require acknowledgement of the same monotonic generation and roll back on failure.

Rejected: one-shot current-input mute, toggle-owned subscriptions, audio-service restarts, and unacknowledged apply.

08Early prooftask .1
Task .1 must prove live service resolution and exact-row behavior with identical-summary member/non-member toasts; no blocked popup or history file; a member showHistory replay left visible; every shipped PWA banner matched only by its leading origin; and every shipped PWA sound positively identified through leaf/ancestor ancestry. It must also prove one listen-owned watcher receives matching generation acknowledgements and catches new streams. If normal PWA identity or per-app isolation fails, stop before count work rather than widen to summary, DND, or all Chromium.
